## Project Management Process Overview

OctoAcme employs a structured, collaborative project management system designed to align teams, define clear roles, and ensure consistent delivery of product features and improvements. The process begins with project initiation, where stakeholders validate business needs, success criteria, and align on deliverables through a standardized one-pager. Once a project is approved, planning activities break down objectives into actionable backlogs, define timelines, and identify risks and dependencies. Every project has both a Project Manager (PM) and Product Lead, providing clear ownership and responsibility for coordination, risk management, and outcome measurement.

Team roles are distinctly articulated: Project Managers oversee schedules, risk, and communication; Product Managers clarify goals and prioritize the backlog; Developers build, test, and maintain code; QA ensures feature acceptance; and Stakeholders provide feedback and approvals. OctoAcme's communication cadence includes daily standups, weekly syncs, monthly stakeholder updates, and a tiered escalation path for blockers and risks. Risks and dependencies are tracked in a register and communicated via status templates and incident summaries.

Quality assurance is central: new logic is covered by unit, integration, and smoke tests, with security scanning in CI pipelines. Releases follow strict checklists—ensuring criteria are met, with rollback plans and post-deploy verifications. Retrospectives are held after each milestone or incident, with action items tracked for continuous improvement. This combination of roles, communication, and testing enables repeatable success and an open, learning-focused team culture.
